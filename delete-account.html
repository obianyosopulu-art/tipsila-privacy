Account & Data Deletion

Delete Your Tipsila Account

Tipsila users can request deletion of their account and associated personal data.

This page explains how to submit an account deletion request and what information will be deleted.

How to Request Account Deletion

To request deletion of your Tipsila account:

  1. Send an email from the email address associated with your Tipsila account.
  2. Use the subject: Tipsila Account Deletion Request.
  3. Include the email address associated with your Tipsila account.
  4. State clearly that you want your Tipsila account and associated personal data deleted.

Send the request to:

tipsila.sops@gmail.com

Data That Will Be Deleted

When an eligible account deletion request is completed, information associated with the account may include:

  • Tipsila account information
  • User profile information
  • Prediction history
  • Prediction performance information
  • Rewarded-ad unlock progress
  • Notification preferences
  • Device notification tokens associated with the account
  • Other user-specific application data stored by Tipsila

Subscription Information

Deleting your Tipsila account does not necessarily cancel an active Google Play subscription.

If you have an active Tipsila subscription, you should also manage or cancel the subscription through your Google Play account.

Information That May Be Retained

Certain limited information may be retained where necessary for:

  • Legal or regulatory obligations
  • Fraud prevention
  • Security and abuse prevention
  • Resolving disputes
  • Enforcing applicable agreements

Information processed independently by third-party service providers may also be subject to their own retention policies.

For security purposes, Tipsila may need to verify that the person requesting deletion is the owner of the account before completing the request.

Privacy Policy

For more information about how Tipsila handles user information, please read our Privacy Policy.
