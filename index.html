Privacy Policy

Effective Date: August 12, 2026

Tipsila respects your privacy and is committed to protecting your personal information. This Privacy Policy explains how information is collected, used, stored, and protected when you use the Tipsila Android application.

By using Tipsila, you agree to the practices described in this Privacy Policy.

1. Information We Collect

Tipsila may collect the following information:

  • Account information such as your email address and basic profile information when you sign in.
  • Firebase user identification information associated with your account.
  • Prediction history, including generated Daily and Weekend predictions.
  • Rewarded-ad progress used to determine whether you have completed the required number of ads to unlock a prediction.
  • Subscription and access status for Daily Pro and Weekend Pro.
  • Device notification tokens used to send push notifications.
  • Technical and app usage information required for operation, security, diagnostics, and improvement of the application.
Tipsila does not hold betting funds, accept wagers, process gambling deposits, or pay betting winnings.

2. Predictions and Sports Information

Tipsila provides sports prediction information for informational and entertainment purposes.

Predictions displayed by Tipsila are not guarantees of future results. Users are responsible for decisions they make based on information provided through the application.

Tipsila does not act as a bookmaker, betting operator, betting wallet, or gambling payment service.

3. Firebase Services

Tipsila uses Google Firebase services to operate important parts of the application.

Firebase may be used for:

  • User authentication
  • Cloud Firestore database storage
  • Secure application data
  • User prediction history
  • Reward unlock progress
  • Notification preferences
  • Device notification tokens
  • Backend Cloud Functions

Information processed through Firebase may be handled according to Google's applicable privacy and security practices.

4. Advertising

Tipsila uses Google AdMob to display advertisements.

Certain features may allow or require users to voluntarily watch rewarded advertisements before accessing specific prediction content.

Google AdMob may process information including:

  • Device information
  • Advertising identifiers
  • IP address
  • Advertisement interaction information
  • Diagnostic and performance information

Advertising information is processed according to Google's applicable privacy policies.

Tipsila grants an unlock step only after the advertising provider confirms that the applicable rewarded advertisement has been successfully completed.

5. Subscriptions and Google Play Billing

Tipsila may offer optional paid subscriptions including Daily Pro and Weekend Pro.

Eligible subscriptions may remove the requirement to watch rewarded advertisements for applicable features.

Subscription purchases and payments are processed through Google Play Billing.

Tipsila does not directly receive or store your complete payment card details.

6. Push Notifications

Tipsila may send notifications relating to:

  • Daily prediction availability
  • Weekend prediction availability
  • Prediction result updates
  • Account or service information
  • Optional subscription or promotional information

Users can manage notification preferences within Tipsila or through their Android device settings.

Firebase Cloud Messaging may be used to deliver these notifications.

7. Prediction History and Performance

Tipsila may store generated predictions under your account so that you can access your prediction history.

Tipsila may calculate performance statistics using individual prediction results.

Pending and void selections may be excluded from win-rate calculations.

8. How We Use Information

Information may be used to:

  • Create and manage user accounts
  • Provide requested predictions
  • Save prediction history
  • Maintain rewarded-ad unlock progress
  • Calculate performance statistics
  • Manage subscription access
  • Send notifications
  • Improve reliability and functionality
  • Prevent abuse, fraud, and unauthorized activity
  • Maintain application security
  • Comply with applicable legal obligations

9. Sharing of Information

Tipsila does not sell users' personal information.

Information may be processed by service providers required for operation of the application, including:

  • Google Firebase
  • Google AdMob
  • Google Play Billing
  • Firebase Cloud Messaging

Information may also be disclosed where required by applicable law, regulation, legal process, or valid governmental request.

10. Data Security

Tipsila uses reasonable technical and organizational safeguards designed to protect user information.

Services including Firebase Authentication, Firestore security rules, and secure backend services may be used to restrict unauthorized access.

No internet-based service can guarantee absolute security.

11. Data Retention

User information may be retained for as long as necessary to provide Tipsila services, maintain account history, protect the platform, resolve disputes, and comply with applicable legal requirements.

12. Account and Data Deletion

Users may request deletion of their Tipsila account and associated personal information.

Where account deletion is available directly inside the application, users may use that functionality.

Users may also contact Tipsila regarding an account or data deletion request.

Certain information may be retained when necessary for legal, security, fraud-prevention, or regulatory purposes.

13. Children's Privacy

Tipsila is not intended for children below the minimum age required to independently use the service under applicable law.

Tipsila does not knowingly seek to collect children's personal information in violation of applicable law.
